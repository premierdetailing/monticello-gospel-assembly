What we believe

We’re a small vibrant church in Lenexa, Kansas. We believe in having church in a way that allows the Spirit of the Lord to speak to the church. There may be teaching, preaching, testimonies, prayer and worship. Each service is different, but it is our desire to be led by the Spirit!

It all starts with Faith and Repentance…

“The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel.”

Mark 1:15

“Testifying both to the Jews, and also to the Greeks, repentance toward God, and faith toward our Lord Jesus Christ.”

Acts 20:21

“Then said Paul, John verily baptized with the baptism of repentance, saying unto the people, that they should believe on him which should come after him, that is, on Christ Jesus.”

Acts 19:4

John the Baptist prepared the way of the Lord with his repentance and water baptism message. This step in salvation did not stop with him, but was carried on by the early church. Even Jesus, without sin, had John baptize him “to fulfill all righteousness”!

“And now why tarriest thou? arise, and be baptized, and wash away thy sins, calling on the name of the Lord.”

Acts 22:16

“He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.”

Mark 16:16

“Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to Jordan unto John, to be baptized of him. But John forbad him, saying, I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me? And Jesus answering said unto him, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness. Then he suffered him.”

Matthew 3:13–15

After Jesus’ death, burial and resurrection, He commanded them to tarry in Jerusalem, and wait on the promise…

“And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.”

Acts 2:4

“And they of the circumcision which believed were astonished, as many as came with Peter, because that on the Gentiles also was poured out the gift of the Holy Ghost. For they heard them speak with tongues, and magnify God.”

Acts 10:45–46a

We believe that same gift is still for anyone who desires it today.

“In the last day, that great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried, saying, If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink. He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.”

John 7:37–38

“For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call.”

Acts 2:39

Getting back to what Jesus said

After the Apostles were martyred, most of what Jesus taught the apostles, with the passage of time, was lost or buried under tradition. We want to get back to it, plain and simple, the way it was given.

Jesus taught the Apostles the truth. We find much of the “red letter” applying to the “principles”, the foundation, or bedrock of the church. However, Jesus taught His Apostles other foundational truths that we find revelation of, throughout the scriptures.

“Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection; not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God, of the doctrine of baptisms, and of laying on of hands, and of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.”

Hebrews 6:1–2

As long as the church continued on in the truth, apostasy was prevented!

“And they continued stedfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers. And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles. And all that believed were together, and had all things common.”

Acts 2:42–44

However, the Apostles had to warn the early church that there were those in authority with the wrong spirit coming among them: "spiritual wickedness in high places"…

“For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock. Also of your own selves shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them.”

Acts 20:29–30

This “mystery of iniquity” started as a whisper, but grew more prevalent as the Apostles passed off the scene. By the time Jude wrote his letter (65-70 AD), his appeal was apparent…

“Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints.”

Jude 1:3

“Once” delivered. By this time, Jude knew many were deceiving and being deceived by false ideologies and heresies that had already crept in. Paul had prophesied of this years earlier in his second letter to the Thessalonians (50-51 AD)…

“Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first.”

2 Thessalonians 2:3a

That glorious church fell away, but not a moment before it accomplished the harvesting work that the Lord had commissioned it to do! John prophesied in Matt 3…

“I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire: Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.”

Matthew 3:11-12

Apostles Paul and Peter further elaborated…

“But ye are come unto mount Sion, and unto the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to an innumerable company of angels, To the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven, and to God the Judge of all, and to the spirits of just men made perfect…”

Hebrews 12:22-23

“For the time is come that judgment must begin at the house of God: and if it first begin at us, what shall the end be of them that obey not the gospel of God?”

1 Peter 4:17

Scriptures prophesied and history verified that the church continued its decline into a world of man’s tradition mixing with pagan religion, a poorly constructed counterfeit of what the true church was called to be…

The good news! Restoration started in the Dark Ages with a ministry that became unsettled by a revelation of a fundamental truth! Martin Luther was a well-known example of this period of restoration. There’s more to this story, but the Lord has been building, is building, and will continue to build His church! We are part of a progressive work that will culminate in a glorious church like the first: the collective representation of Christ at the close of this Gentile age!

One body

The church isn’t a building or a name on a sign. It’s the people God puts together.

Jesus prayed in St. John 17 “that they may be one, as we are.” This intimate prayer expresses the deepest desires of Jesus’ heart for the church. Jesus Christ is building the church, and we are called to be one body, unified in Christ.

“For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit.”

1 Corinthians 12:12-13

“There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.”

Ephesians 4:4-6

“And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.”

Colossians 3:15

That body must take on the headship of Christ. Any other head but Jesus Christ, would make the body in question, some other body.

“And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.”

Colossians 1:18

“And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church, Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all.”

Ephesians 1:22-23

“Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works.”

Titus 2:14

Getting ready

The same Lord who began the work will be the One who finishes it. Salvation is not a project we start and then leave to our own strength to complete. God does not abandon what He begins. He keeps shaping, correcting and drawing us closer, even in the seasons when we cannot see the progress.

“Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ.”

Philippians 1:6

With this in mind, we also have a great part in the salvation plan. We are called to produce fruit, as the planting of the Lord. We are involved in a process that will produce an overcoming life, if we remain faithful.

“And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.”

Romans 12:2

“For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world.”

Titus 2:11–12

Through the influence of God’s Word and the work of His Spirit, we can be changed from a carnal man into a new creation in Christ!

“That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; and be renewed in the spirit of your mind; and that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.”

Ephesians 4:22–24

“But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord.”

2 Corinthians 3:18

Jesus is coming back, and He’s coming for a church that’s ready.

“The marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready.”

Revelation 19:7
“Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.”
1 Peter 5:7
